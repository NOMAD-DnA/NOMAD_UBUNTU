# SunSet skybox

The six unmodified SunSet JPEG textures are from **SkyboxSet by Heiko Irrgang**,
based on work at 93i.de. They are licensed under **Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ .

Original author links: http://gamvas.com/ and http://93i.de/ .
MVSim's official example asset location:
https://mrpt.github.io/mvsim-models/skyboxes/SunSet.zip .

These files were copied unchanged from the already-downloaded local MVSim
example cache. The original LICENSE.txt and README.txt are included in SunSet/.
No new network download or runtime cache lookup is required by the NOMAD map.

The skybox is a visual background. It does not add a Box2D body, a surrounding
collision wall, or extra trees. The existing terrain, roads, vehicle and sensor
configuration are unchanged.
